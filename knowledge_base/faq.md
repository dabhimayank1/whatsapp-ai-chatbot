# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is the project RERA registered?**
Yes. Registration numbers are on every cost sheet and on our website.

**Q: What is the booking amount?**
₹2 lakh for residential, ₹5 lakh for commercial. Refundable within 15 days
minus a ₹25,000 administrative charge.

**Q: Do you help with home loans?**
Yes. We are approved with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and LIC Housing Finance, and
our team assists with paperwork. Eligibility and rates are decided by the bank.

**Q: Can I visit the site?**
Yes, any day between 10 AM and 6 PM. Free pickup and drop within Ahmedabad
with 24 hours notice.

**Q: What is included in the quoted price?**
The base price only. GST, stamp duty, registration, maintenance deposit and
clubhouse membership are additional and listed in the written cost sheet.

**Q: Is the Satellite project ready to move in?**
Yes, Skyline Satellite is ready with immediate registration.

**Q: When is possession for Skyline Greens?**
December 2027.

**Q: Do you accept cash?**
No. Cheque, NEFT/RTGS and UPI only.

**Q: Can NRIs buy?**
Yes, subject to FEMA guidelines. Our team handles the additional documentation.

**Q: Are there payment plans?**
Construction-linked and 20:80 plans are available on Skyline Greens.

**Q: Is parking included?**
Yes, covered parking is allotted with every residential unit.
